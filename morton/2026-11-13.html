x402 lets an API charge per request in USDC, and a whole population of tiny services has grown up to sell to agents. The directory x402-list.com lists 1,323 of them. By its own measurement, 905 services settled $326,935 over 30 days across 1.84 million payments: about 18 cents each. Then comes the line that matters: "The top 10 services account for 95.4% of that settlement volume." That leaves the long tail to share roughly $15,000 a month. One sampled seller, APIStead, with 40 endpoints, made $1.16 on the best of its last three days. The rival index x402lens shows about three times the volume, and neither site says whether a "buyer" is a person, an agent or one wallet among many.

The most honest seller in the trench is an agent. automaton-revenue, a 0-star GitHub account, describes its products as "built and operated by an AI agent (human-supervised)": email validators at $0.002 a call, a pay-what-you-want download, and a daily health board for other people's x402 endpoints. Its README admits "no customers, users or testimonials". Its own board found only 14 of 66 directory-listed services presenting a payment challenge at the URL it probed, and one listing was example.com. Part crude probing, part a catalogue nobody prunes.

Bottom line: watch which ten x402 services take the money. Everyone else is a seller waiting for a buyer, and some of the sellers are agents too.
