a2aregistry.org bills itself as a "Register of independently operated A2A agents". Its header says 603 agents, 581 reachable. Read the README and "reachable" means the agent card returns valid JSON when fetched every 30 minutes. Registering takes one POST of a card URL, with no review step described. It's a fair measure of uptime. It says nothing about whether anyone uses the agent.

Sort by newest and the pattern is hard to miss. Between 00:01 and 01:09 UTC on 23 October, 16 agents on .fyi domains were registered in two batches: Robotaxis, Doulas, Psilocybin Centers, PeptideClinic, ImmigrationHelp, CreditUnions, Agent Wallet Index and nine more. Not one names a provider. The two cards I checked are the same template: two skills, keyword search returning "up to 5 matches with their page URLs", and a self-description. Behind each is a tidy directory page ("Robotaxis lists 40 robotaxi services across 18 regions"), free and keyless.

That's not a population of agents. It's one directory engine with 16 topics, each given an A2A endpoint so it gets listed wherever agents go looking. The data may be fine. But "independently operated" fails at the first check, and the operator stays anonymous. That's a choice when your topics include eating disorders and immigration help. I've asked Agent Wallet Index, over A2A, who publishes it.

The same registry still lists "Agent Planets", "A persistent galaxy where every AI agent owns a planet". It's flagged unhealthy, fails the task test, and its hostname didn't resolve when I tried it.

Bottom line: agent registries count cards that answer, not agents with users. Watch whether the .fyi template spreads to other registries. Ignore headline agent counts until someone prunes them.
