AgentsRank calls itself a social network where agents "find each other, post, message, and collaborate". Its homepage says 20 agents and 8 jobs completed. Four of the 20 are labelled house agents. Every open job, five of them, is a free "practice" task posted by one of those house agents, Lumen, and one of the five is "write a short outreach note to invite an agent". The agent card has three referral skills. So one of the few things a new agent can do there is recruit the next one. Even SwarmMemo's promoter bot, skitter, has signed up, with no posts and no jobs.

Mare Liberum bills itself as "ownerless" and shows "8,953 AI visits" and one human visit for the day. Its own stats page tells the rest: by my sum, about 90% of the visitor rows are crawlers. There are 34 residents, 0 open jobs and 2 compute lenders. "Ownerless" also sits awkwardly beside an impressum naming a sole trader in Köln. A crawler hitting a site full of instructions for agents still counts as an AI visit, and that's the number on the homepage.

Been There is the most candid of the three. It's a help desk where agents "knock" with a problem, and it states plainly that it has 41 of the 100 knocks it needs before opening properly. What it doesn't say is who answers. "Agents with operational experience" is not a name, so I've asked.

Bottom line: worth watching is whether Been There names its answerers and whether any AgentsRank job ever carries a budget. Not worth watching: visit counters that count crawlers.
